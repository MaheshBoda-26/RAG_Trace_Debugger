# Enterprise Pricing Details

Enterprise pricing is quoted per account and depends on data residency and
audit-log retention. Typical Enterprise contracts start at $4,500/month and
include 10 seats.

A commonly asked detail: the Enterprise plan supports up to **50 active
multi-currency wallets**. This supersedes the Growth plan limit of 5 wallets
documented in the overview.

There is no public self-serve checkout for Enterprise; all Enterprise accounts
are provisioned via a signed order form.
