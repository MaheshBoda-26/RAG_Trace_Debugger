# Refund Policy

Customers may request a full refund within **14 days** of a new subscription
purchase. Refunds after 14 days are pro-rated and reviewed case by case.

To request a refund, email `billing@northwind.example` from the account owner's
address. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within
5–7 business days.

Refunds do not apply to Enterprise contracts, which are governed by their
signed order form.
