# Northwind SaaS Overview

Northwind SaaS is a subscription platform for small finance teams. The product
has three plans: **Starter**, **Growth**, and **Enterprise**. This document is
a general overview; pricing details live in `pricing.md` and `pricing_enterprise.md`.

Customers on the Growth plan get access to automated bank reconciliation,
multi-currency wallets (up to 5 active wallets), and email support with a
business-hours response target of 4 hours.

The Enterprise plan adds a dedicated success manager, SSO via SAML, and a
custom data residency option.
