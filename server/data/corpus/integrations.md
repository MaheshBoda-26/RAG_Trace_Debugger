# Integrations

Northwind SaaS integrates with the following accounting and banking tools:

- **QuickBooks Online** — two-way sync of invoices and payments.
- **Xero** — one-way export of settled transactions.
- **Stripe** — import of payouts and automatic reconciliation.
- **Plaid** — bank feed ingestion for reconciliation.

The QuickBooks integration requires the QuickBooks Online **Advanced** tier;
the standard tier is not supported and sync will silently fail.
